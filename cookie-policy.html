Cookie Policy

Our approach

This website sets no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Strictly necessary

The site does not need cookies to work. Fonts and images are served from our own server.

Google Maps (only after your click)

The map in the Location section is loaded from Google only when you click “Show map”. Google may then set its own cookies under the Google privacy policy. If you don't click, nothing is loaded from Google.

Booking engine

The official booking engine (villamorea.hotelyzer.gr) opens in a new tab and may set its own cookies, as described in its own policy.

Managing cookies

You can view and delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. If analytics are added to this site in the future, a consent banner will be shown first and this policy will be updated.
