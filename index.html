Overview

A quiet villa in Chania with a pool just for you

Villa Morea is located in Mournies, a quiet neighbourhood just outside Chania town, with private parking and a private swimming pool.

Four bedrooms that sleep up to eight guests and a living room corner sofa bed that brings the total to nine guests. The dining area and kitchen lead out to the pool terrace, which is perfect for outdoor dining. Chania's historic centre is about 4 km away and the nearest beaches about 5 km.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can Villa Morea accommodate?

Up to 9 guests: three bedrooms with queen beds, one bedroom with a bunk bed, and a corner sofa bed in the living room.

Is the swimming pool private?

Yes. The pool is used only by guests of Villa Morea during their stay.

How far is the villa from Chania and the beach?

Chania's Old Town and Venetian Harbour are about 4 km away and the nearest beach (Nea Chora) about 5 km.

How far is Chania Airport?

Chania International Airport (CHQ) is about 20 km away, roughly 25–30 minutes by car.

Do we need a car?

We recommend one. Free private parking for up to 3 cars is available at the villa, and a car makes day trips to Balos, Falasarna, Elafonisi or the Samaria Gorge easy.

Is Wi-Fi included?

Yes, free Wi-Fi (around 88 Mbps) and a dedicated workspace.

Is the residence private?

Yes. The first floor is rented exclusively to your group, with private access via an external staircase. A relative of the owner lives on the lower level and does not use the pool or the guest areas; their access may occasionally cross the outdoor area.

Is Villa Morea suitable for families?

Yes. The bunk-bed bedroom and the sofa bed work well for families, and the garden gives children room to play. Please supervise children around the pool at all times.
