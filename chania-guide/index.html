Chania guide: what to see and do near Villa Morea

Villa Morea sits in Mournies, 4 km from Chania. From here, western Crete is easy to explore — from Venetian lanes to some of the island's most famous beaches.

Chania Old Town & Venetian Harbour

From the villa: 4 km

Chania's Old Town is one of the most beautiful in Greece: a maze of narrow lanes, Venetian mansions and Ottoman details wrapped around the old harbour. Walk along the harbour front to the lighthouse, explore the Splantzia and Topanas quarters, and stay for dinner by the water.

Tip: the drive from the villa takes about 10–15 minutes. Park outside the Old Town walls and walk in.

Beaches near Chania

From the villa: 5–15 km

The closest sandy beach is Nea Chora, a short drive from the villa and a walk from the Old Town. Heading west along the coast you'll find Chrissi Akti, Agioi Apostoloi with its sheltered coves, Kalamaki and the long beaches of Agia Marina and Platanias, all with tavernas and sunbeds.

On the Akrotiri peninsula, east of Chania, Stavros beach sits in a shallow, calm bay, while Seitan Limania is a dramatic narrow cove reached by a short, steep path.

Balos Lagoon & Gramvousa

From the villa: ~60 km

Balos is the postcard image of Crete: a shallow turquoise lagoon at the tip of the Gramvousa peninsula. You can reach it by boat from Kissamos port, often combined with the Venetian fortress on Gramvousa island, or by car on a dirt road followed by a walk down to the beach.

Go early in the day in summer, bring water and shade, and allow a full day.

Falasarna

From the villa: ~55 km

On the west coast, Falasarna is a long stretch of fine sand and clear water, famous for its sunsets. It's a relaxed full-day trip, easy to combine with Kissamos.

Elafonisi

From the villa: ~75 km

In the far south-west, Elafonisi is known for its shallow lagoon and pink-tinted sand. It is a protected natural area, so please stay on the paths and leave the sand and shells where they are. The scenic drive passes through mountain villages and chestnut forests.

Samaria Gorge

From the villa: ~40 km to Omalos

One of Europe's best-known hikes: about 16 km from the Omalos plateau down to Agia Roumeli on the Libyan Sea, then a ferry and bus back. The national park usually opens from spring to autumn, depending on weather. Wear proper shoes and start early.

Organised day tours with pick-up from Chania are an easy option if you don't want to drive.

Getting around

A car is the easiest way to explore western Crete, and the villa has free private parking for up to 3 cars. Chania International Airport (CHQ) is about 20 km away and Souda port about 8 km.

All distances are approximate driving distances from Villa Morea in Mournies.
